## Instructions for Adding to Admin Dashboard

1. **Go to Admin Dashboard** → **Policy Documents** tab
2. **Create New Version** for each document:
   - **For Terms of Service:**
     - Policy Type: Terms of Service
     - Version: 1.0
     - Title: Terms of Service v1.0
     - Content: Copy the Terms of Service HTML content above
   
   - **For Privacy Policy:**
     - Policy Type: Privacy Policy
     - Version: 1.0
     - Title: Privacy Policy v1.0
     - Content: Copy the Privacy Policy HTML content above

3. **Publish** each document after creating it
4. The documents will automatically appear on the public `/terms` and `/privacy` pages

**Note:** The HTML uses Tailwind CSS classes that match your existing design system. The content will render properly with dark mode support.
